About Campus Care AI

Understanding our mission and privacy commitments.

Our Mission

Campus Care AI was developed to provide an accessible, immediate, and non-judgmental space for college students to vent, seek emotional support, and find resources.


College life can be incredibly stressful, and sometimes you just need someone to listen. While our AI is empathetic, it is designed to guide you toward real human help when you need it.

Strict Privacy

Your privacy is our top priority.

  • No Tracking: We do not ask for your name, email, or student ID.
  • No Databases: Your chats are NOT stored in any database. Once you close the tab, the conversation is gone forever.
  • Confidential: Your inputs are processed in real-time and immediately discarded.

Important Disclaimer

Not a Medical Professional: This AI assistant provides non-clinical emotional support only. It cannot diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you are in crisis, please use our Helplines page immediately.
